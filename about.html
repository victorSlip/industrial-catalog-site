Our history

Nordtec Machinery, founded in Stuttgart in 2004, designs machines and accessories for industrial surface finishing. What started as a small workshop building polishing units is today a supplier of complete finishing lines, sold directly and through partners in several countries.

2004 – Founding of Nordtec Machinery

Nordtec is founded in Stuttgart, Germany, to build compact and reliable polishing machines for local metalworking companies.

2011 – First installations abroad

With a new export department, Nordtec delivers complete finishing lines to customers in Europe, Asia and South America.

2019 – Connected machines and service network

Nordtec launches its line of connected machines with remote diagnostics and builds a partner network for fast on-site service.
